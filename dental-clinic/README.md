# 🦷 DentalCare Management System

A web-based platform designed to simplify dental clinic operations, manage patient records, streamline appointment scheduling, and track treatment histories efficiently.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Patient Management:** Easily add, update, and manage patient profiles and medical history.
* **Appointment Scheduling:** Book, reschedule, or cancel dental appointments seamlessly.
* **Treatment & Prescription Tracking:** Record diagnosis, ongoing dental procedures, and prescribed medications.
* **Billing & Invoicing:** Generate clear billing summaries for procedures.
* **Admin Dashboard:** Overview of daily appointments, revenue, and active patients.
