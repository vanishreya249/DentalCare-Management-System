Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our practice and procedures.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most major PPO dental insurance plans. Our front desk team will be happy to verify your benefits before your appointment and help you maximize your coverage.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit will include a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays if necessary, and a consultation with the dentist to discuss any concerns and create a personalized treatment plan.

Do you offer payment plans?

Absolutely. We partner with CareCredit and offer flexible, interest-free financing options to ensure your dental care fits within your budget.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We are always excited to welcome new patients to our dental family. You can easily book your first appointment through our website or by giving us a call.
